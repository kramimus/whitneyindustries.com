MW Goo Bot

Personal automation assistant for the Whitney household.

What this app does

MW Goo Bot is a private, single-user automation assistant. It connects to the account mwgoobot@gmail.com and acts only on the owner's explicit instruction:

  • Gmail — search, read, draft, send, and reply to email on request
  • Calendar — read and create events and reminders
  • Drive & Docs — find and export documents
  • Sheets — read and update spreadsheets
  • Contacts & Tasks — look up contacts and manage tasks

How it works

The app runs on the household's own private server. It accesses Google services through OAuth 2.0 with narrowly scoped permissions granted by the account owner. It serves exactly one user (the owner), does not offer public sign-up, and does not share data with any third party.

Privacy

This app handles Google account data. Read the privacy policy for details on what is accessed, how it is used, and how to revoke access at any time.
