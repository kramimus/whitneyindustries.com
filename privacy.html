Privacy Policy

MW Goo Bot · Effective September 13, 2026 · Contact: mwgoobot@gmail.com

Overview

MW Goo Bot ("the app") is a personal automation assistant operated by the Whitney household for private, non-commercial use. This policy explains how the app handles data associated with Google accounts, including Google user data accessed through Google APIs.

What Google data the app accesses

With the account owner's OAuth 2.0 authorization, the app may access:

  • Gmail — message content and metadata (searching, reading, drafting, sending, and replying to email on the owner's instruction)
  • Google Calendar — calendars and events
  • Google Drive & Docs — files and documents
  • Google Sheets — spreadsheets and their contents
  • Contacts — contact records
  • Tasks — task lists and items

How the app uses this data

  • Data is used solely to carry out instructions given by the account owner (for example: "summarize today's mail" or "add this event to my calendar").
  • The app has a single user — the owner. There is no public sign-up, no multi-user service, and no advertising.
  • Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training third-party models.

How data is stored and protected

  • The app runs on a private server controlled by the owner. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on that server with restricted access.
  • Email and document content is processed transiently to fulfill a specific instruction; the app does not build a separate permanent copy of your Google data.
  • Access to the server is limited to the household operator.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Revoking access immediately prevents the app from obtaining new tokens; existing credentials are then deleted from the server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
